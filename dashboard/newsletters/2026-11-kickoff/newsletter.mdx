# Welcome to our first newsletter

Hi! My name is Marley, I am the lead for Haav, a student boat team at UW-Madison, competing in PEP27. You have been added to this email newsletter if you have supported, advised, or helped us in any way. Thank you!! Below is the basic overview of the competition, our strategy and our progress.


## The competition constraints

A planing hull carrying a 30 pound added payload over a 2 mile course. Fastest time wins. Must be electric, under 55V, and have positive buoyancy.

## Strategy

- Test early and often
- Don't reinvent the wheel, learn from the coffins (past teams). Fix the bottlenecks they had.
- Data and software can give us a huge leg up, use it to accelerate hardware.

## Our progress
- We made a model boat and it floats! This model boat will serve as our test platform for autonomy, GPS, IMU and communication systems, as well as a place to see basic feedback on hull design. A big shoutout to Kevin for letting us use the pool at the Marine Robotics Lab.

## Asks
Adding a few specific things would make a real difference right now. Reply here if you would be willing to support or have any guidance on who to reach out to.

1. **Design reviews.** We have an internal design review this Friday. We would love to do design reviews with you early next week, before Thanksgiving, if you are willing. Let me know and we can find a time that works and I will send the CAD and our slides.
2. **Shaft advice.** We need advice on our propeller shaft: sizing, materials, bearings, and sealing where it passes through the hull. If you have done this before, even a short conversation would help.
3. **Parts.** We are ordering critical and long-lead parts this month. If you have, or can get us a good price on, Raspberry Pi, waterproof enclosures (Pelican Case), Foam please let us know.
4. **Donations.** Cash lets us buy the parts we cannot get donated. Any amount helps, and we are happy to put your name or logo in front of the team, and all donations are tax deductible since they are donated through the University of Wisconsin.
